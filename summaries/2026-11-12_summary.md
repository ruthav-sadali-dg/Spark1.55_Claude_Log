# Session Summaries – 2026-11-12

## 20261112-001: Location of the TB2 10A fuse used in the DI1-IN2 (30A ATS Status) test

**Question:** In SPK26-000021 MDC_FAT_Tracker_v1.1, the DI1-IN2 test says "open the TB2 10A
fuse that supplies the ATS cabinet". Is that TB2 fuse in the marshalling cabinet?

**Answer: Yes.** TB2 is the marshalling cabinet's 24V power distribution terminal block.
- The tracker doesn't say where TB2 is. Outstanding Item OI-10(a) lists "the TB2 fuse that
  feeds the ATS" as open/unconfirmed.
- Drawing **E-340-01366 Rev H, sheet 3 of 8** (Marshalling Cabinet Power Distribution - TB1, TB2,
  TB4) shows **TB2 "24V PWR DIST"** in the marshalling cabinet. **TB2 FU.2-10A** (wire C.105, +)
  and **TB2 2.B** (C.106, -) feed the **ATS cabinet (E-340-00934) TB1.FU.1(+) / TB1.1(-)** over
  cable **C-ATS-1, pair 10**.
- Sheet 4 of 8: the DI1-IN2 input loop is powered separately, from TB2 FU.10 → TB-DI1 FU.2 (1A) →
  C.210B → ATS TB4 1-2 (ATS Status contact) → C.210A → TB-DI1 2.B → DI1 IN-2. So opening TB2 FU.2
  removes the ATS cabinet's 24V. The status contact drops, and IN-2 goes OFF while its own loop
  stays powered. That matches the tracker's "OFF = DC power lost" and the 11/09 PASS.

**Method (reusable):** For any "which fuse / where" question, go from the tracker row to the
marshalling drawing E-340-01366. Sheet 3 is power distribution (TB1/TB2/TB4), and sheets 4-7 are the DI cards.
Trace the field cable label (C-ATS-1, C-CBB1-1, etc.) to the destination cabinet.

**Not verified:** That ATS TB1 actually powers the ATS status relay/controller. That's in the ATS
cabinet drawing E-340-00934, which isn't in Downloads.
