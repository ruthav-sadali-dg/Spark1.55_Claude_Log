
## 20261118-001: XT7 breaker closed feedback not reaching the PLC, but HMI shows Closed

**Goal:** Explain why the HMI shows an ABB Tmax XT7 breaker as Closed when the hardwired closed-feedback signal is absent.

**Findings (from the repo):** In the Tmax UDT (`EPMS/Systems/Trip Units/Equipment Modules/ABB/Tmax/v1_0_1`), `Status/BKRSTS` reads the trip unit over Modbus (`IRUS40.0`). `Status/BKRSTSWPLC` uses BKRSTS whenever `Comms/COMMSTS` is Connected and its quality is good. It only falls back to the hardwired PLC tag `.../Diagnostics/PLC/Marshalling/<CB>/Breaker_Closed` when comms are lost. So a dead aux contact is invisible on the HMI until the Modbus link drops; then the HMI would wrongly show Open.

**Field checklist (SOP draft):** Compare BKRSTS with the PLC `Breaker_Closed` tag in the Tag Browser. Check the PLC DI LED with the breaker closed and then open. If the DI is ON when the breaker is open, an NC contact is wired instead of NO. Check that the open/closed aux contact (not the trip-signal contact) is fitted and wired. On withdrawable units, check the auxiliary connector is engaged. Check the DI common/24 V supply, fuse, and marshalling terminations. If an Ekip Signalling output relay is used, check that it is programmed for "CB closed" and that its aux power is present. Field work by qualified electricians only (arc-flash PPE / LOTO).

**Outcome:** Explanation and checklist given. Offered a discrepancy alarm (BKRSTS vs Breaker_Closed).

**Follow-up (Ekip Signalling 2K-3):** The closed feedback comes from an Ekip Signalling 2K-3 output relay, not a mechanical aux contact. Checks: confirm the trip unit detects the 2K-3 module. Confirm the 2K-3 outputs (typically O31/O32) have a signal source of CB Closed / CB status, contact NO, no delay, no latch. Make sure the config is on the 2K-3 and not on 2K-1/2K-2, and the field wiring lands on the 2K-3 terminals. The 2K outputs are dry contacts, so the PLC side must supply its 24 V through them. Use Ekip Connect to view or test output states. Aux power is very likely fine, since Modbus (Ekip Com) is working.
