**Key facts (for reuse as a quick reference):**
- $70.00/day per diem covers meals, laundry, incidentals, and reasonable personal cell phone use while traveling to do project work.
- Eligibility: only for project travel that lasts more than one day **and** when the client reimburses per diem. Trips under 24 hours, non-project travel, local clients, and Partners/Directors/non-billable staff submit actual expenses with receipts instead.
- Partial days, or a meal someone else paid for: breakfast 25% ($17.50), lunch 25% ($17.50), dinner 50% ($35.00).
- Per diem counts as taxable income and is reported to payroll each quarter. Keep records of actual costs or the IRS city rates for a personal tax deduction.
- Don't use the corporate card for personal costs that per diem covers.
